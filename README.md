# Abdul Rab

**Lecturer in Mathematics | Numerical Analysis | Numerical PDEs | Scientific Computing**

I am a mathematics lecturer and researcher interested in numerical analysis, differential equations, scientific computing, and mathematical modeling. My current research direction focuses on numerical methods for hyperbolic conservation laws, Riemann problems, and nonlinear PDE systems.

I teach mathematics at Government Islamia Science College, Sukkur, and also serve as Visiting Faculty at Sukkur IBA University.

## Research interests

- Numerical analysis
- Numerical methods for PDEs
- Hyperbolic conservation laws
- Riemann problems and shock-capturing methods
- Finite-volume and finite-difference methods
- Scientific computing
- Mathematical modeling
- Numerical stability, convergence, and error analysis

## Selected research

### Numerical Solution to the Riemann Problem for the Liquid–Gas Two-Phase Isentropic Flow Model
M.S. research focused on numerical approximation of a nonlinear two-phase flow system using Lax–Friedrichs, Lax–Wendroff, and FORCE schemes.

**Topics:** numerical PDEs, Riemann problems, two-phase flow, shock waves, rarefaction waves, Python

[View preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02071)

### Mathematical Modeling and Simulation of HIV Dynamics
B.S. research on within-host HIV models with treatment controls, equilibrium and local stability analysis, and fourth-order Runge–Kutta simulations.

**Topics:** mathematical biology, ODEs, stability analysis, RK4, mathematical modeling

[View preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.13171)
